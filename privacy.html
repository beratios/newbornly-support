Privacy Policy for Newbornly

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Newbornly: Baby Tracker (“Newbornly”, “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

Baby and Tracking Data

Newbornly lets you enter information such as a baby's profile and tracked activities, which may include sleep, feeding, breastfeeding, bottles, diapers, pumping, food, growth, medicines, vaccinations, notes, and related routine information.

Newbornly is designed to store this tracking data locally on your device. We do not operate a Newbornly cloud account or server for storing this baby-tracking history. Deleting the app may therefore delete locally stored app data unless it is otherwise preserved by Apple's device backup or system features.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Newbornly may offer auto-renewable subscriptions and free trials. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Newbornly does not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Third-Party Services

The app may use Apple platform services required to provide app functionality, including App Store and StoreKit services. If third-party SDKs or analytics/subscription services are added or used, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must accurately reflect their data practices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your baby-tracking data. We do not use locally stored baby-tracking data for third-party advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored tracking information remains on the device until you delete individual records, use an available deletion feature, or remove the app. If you need assistance regarding data or privacy, contact us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Newbornly is intended for use by parents and caregivers to track information about a child. It is not intended to be independently used by children to create accounts or submit personal information.

Medical Disclaimer

Newbornly provides organizational, tracking, prediction, and informational features only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Contact a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical concerns.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Newbornly changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
